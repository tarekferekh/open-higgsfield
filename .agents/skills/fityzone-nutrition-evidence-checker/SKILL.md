# FityZone Nutrition Evidence Checker

Read `marketing/brand-profile.md` and `references/evidence-rubric.md`.

## Check the claim

1. Rewrite the statement as precise, answerable claims. Preserve population, dose, duration, comparator, and outcome.
2. Identify whether each claim is educational, clinical, performance-related, commercial, or testimonial-based.
3. Search current primary and authoritative sources. Prioritize guidelines, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, consensus statements, and relevant randomized trials.
4. Open and inspect the sources. Do not rely on snippets, headlines, or one study when broader evidence exists.
5. Record source date, population, intervention, outcome, limitations, and direct relevance.
6. Grade the evidence and decide: supported, supported with qualification, uncertain, misleading, or unsupported.

## Rewrite safely

Produce the original claim, verdict, confidence, plain-language summary, limitations, safer publishable wording, necessary disclaimer, source links, and access date.

Distinguish association from causation. Do not generalize athlete, animal, cell, or disease-specific findings to everyone. Avoid diagnosis, treatment promises, guaranteed outcomes, unsafe cutting or dehydration advice, and individualized medical instructions. Escalate clinical or high-risk questions to an appropriately qualified clinician.

## Record

Add approved claims and sources to the relevant brief or evidence log. Include a review date for fast-changing guidance. Never convert an anecdote into scientific proof.

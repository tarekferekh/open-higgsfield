# FityZone Testimonial and Consent Manager

Read `marketing/brand-profile.md` and `references/proof-record-schema.md`.

## Intake

1. Create one proof record per client story and assign a non-public identifier.
2. Store original assets, dates, measurements, quote source, program context, and evidence owner.
3. Record exactly what the client approved: asset types, channels, name visibility, edits, paid advertising, geographic scope, start date, expiry, and withdrawal method.
4. Treat silence, assumption, prior posting, or general coaching consent as insufficient for marketing use. A generic message such as “you can post them” creates a limited pending record, not publication approval: clarify the exact channel, identity visibility, edit scope, caption/context, paid use, validity period, and withdrawal method before use.
5. Keep health details and identity data to the minimum needed.

## Review before use

Verify that consent is explicit and current; dates and measurements are traceable; visuals are not misleadingly altered; copy does not guarantee results; material context is included; minors have required guardian authorization; and the final draft matches the approved visibility and wording.

Mark the asset **approved**, **limited**, **expired**, **withdrawn**, or **blocked**. **Limited** means the record may be prepared only within its explicit boundaries; it does not authorize publication while required fields remain unclear. Never publish a pending or incomplete record.

## Withdrawal and expiry

On withdrawal, stop new use, record the request and date, and identify active placements for removal or replacement. Obtain renewed consent before reusing expired proof. Preserve an audit trail without retaining unnecessary private material.

Do not publish, message, or contact a client without explicit approval from the owner.

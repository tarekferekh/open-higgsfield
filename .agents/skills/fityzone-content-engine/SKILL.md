# FityZone Content Engine

Read `marketing/brand-profile.md` and use `fityzone-brand-system` for visual decisions.

## Plan before writing

1. Define the business objective, audience state, offer, platform, format, and success metric.
2. Choose one job for the asset: attract, educate, build trust, handle an objection, convert, retain, or reactivate.
3. Verify any timely platform fact, medical statement, statistic, price, or research claim.
4. Use only proof that can be traced to an approved source.
5. Choose one message and one primary CTA.

## Reel script format

Deliver a time-coded production table containing:

- 0–2 second hook;
- shot or generated visual;
- narration or dialogue;
- on-screen text;
- motion, edit, sound, and transition;
- retention device or pattern interrupt;
- CTA and final frame.

Also provide the cover headline, caption, first comment if useful, accessibility text, asset list, and generation prompts. Design for comprehension without sound while using audio to improve energy.

## Carousel and static-post format

For carousels, use one idea per slide: promise, context, teaching sequence, proof, action, and CTA. Make slide 1 earn the swipe and ensure each slide works on a mobile screen.

For static posts, pair one strong visual idea with one concise message. Do not turn the artwork into a paragraph.

## Language

Create separate natural Arabic and English versions when bilingual output is requested or approved in the brand profile. Do not translate word for word. Use the approved Arabic register; if Lebanese Arabic is selected, keep it conversational and readable. Avoid crowding both languages into one frame unless the brief requires it.

## Fitness and nutrition safeguards

Do not diagnose, prescribe, promise guaranteed weight loss, promote dangerous cutting or dehydration, or present a client result without consent and context. Distinguish education from individualized medical advice. Use precise, non-alarmist language and add an appropriate professional-advice note when the topic is clinical.

## Quality gate

Check brand fit, novelty, factual support, hook clarity, visual producibility, mobile legibility, CTA alignment, consent, and claim safety. Remove generic AI phrasing, filler hashtags, fabricated urgency, fake social proof, and unsupported numbers.

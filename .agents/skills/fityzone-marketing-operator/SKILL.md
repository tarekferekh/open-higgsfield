# FityZone Marketing Operator

Read `marketing/brand-profile.md`. Use the other FityZone skills for brand, content, and video work.

## Diagnose

1. Define the business goal and time window.
2. Inspect account positioning, profile conversion path, content mix, consistency, creative quality, distribution, audience signals, and lead handling.
3. Separate reach problems, retention problems, trust problems, offer problems, and conversion problems.
4. Use platform and Metricool data when available. State the date range, sample size, and missing data.
5. Avoid conclusions from one post or vanity metrics alone.

Track reach, non-follower reach, saves, shares, comments, watch time, opening retention, completion rate, profile actions, link actions, DMs, qualified leads, booked calls, and clients.

## Build the plan

Create a calendar in which every item has:

- objective and funnel stage;
- audience and insight;
- format and content pillar;
- hook and creative angle;
- CTA and destination;
- owner, status, and due date;
- primary KPI and test variable;
- dependency on proof, assets, or approval.

Balance authority, education, proof, personality, objection handling, and direct offers. Reuse strong ideas across formats without duplicating the same asset.

## Experiment

Change one important variable at a time where possible. Record the hypothesis, baseline, variant, duration, success threshold, result, and next decision. Compare like-for-like formats and account for sample size.

## Publish safely

Prepare drafts and schedules, but require explicit approval before publishing, sending DMs, contacting leads, starting ads, or changing spend. Verify the final asset, caption, link, tags, timing, consent, and claims immediately before scheduling.

## Report

End each review with: what happened, why it likely happened, what remains uncertain, what to stop, what to continue, and the next three prioritized tests.

# FityZone Trend and Competitor Researcher

Read `marketing/brand-profile.md` and `references/research-log-schema.md`.

## Define the scan

1. State the market, audience, platform, topic, language, time window, and business question.
2. Separate Lebanese competitors, international references, adjacent creators, and platform-wide trends.
3. Use current sources and record observation dates. Treat counts and trends as time-sensitive.
4. Sample enough posts to identify repetition rather than reacting to one viral item.

## Analyze

Capture hook, promise, format, length, first-frame visual, pacing, audio role, proof, CTA, comments, recurring questions, and visible engagement signals. Separate observed facts from inference.

Cluster findings into durable audience problems, repeated creative patterns, local cultural cues, saturated ideas, unmet questions, positioning whitespace, and formats FityZone can produce.

Extract principles, not copies. Do not reproduce a creator's distinctive script, identity, slogan, sequence, proprietary framework, or misleading claim. Respect trademarks, privacy, platform terms, and attribution.

## Recommend

For each opportunity, provide the insight, evidence, FityZone angle, format, differentiation, production difficulty, risk, KPI, and one testable hypothesis. Route nutrition claims through `fityzone-nutrition-evidence-checker` and visual decisions through `fityzone-brand-system`.

Do not publish, schedule, contact competitors, or spend money without explicit approval.

# FityZone project instructions

Use this repository as the production workspace for FityZone brand, content, Reel, and marketing work.

For any brand or marketing task:

1. Read `marketing/brand-profile.md`.
2. Load the smallest relevant skill under `.agents/skills/`.
3. Separate verified facts, observed patterns, and creative proposals.
4. Treat the supplied vector brand bible as the highest authority once it is added.
5. Do not reuse earlier AI assumptions as brand facts.
6. Record durable, approved decisions in `marketing/brand-profile.md`.

Evidence priority:

1. Approved brand bible and original vector assets.
2. Explicit owner decisions.
3. Current owned-channel evidence such as the official Instagram profile.
4. Analytics and campaign results.
5. Inference, clearly labeled as a proposal.

Do not publish, schedule, message leads, or spend money without explicit approval. Keep client identities and results private unless documented consent is available. For fitness, nutrition, and health content, avoid diagnosis, guaranteed outcomes, unsafe protocols, fabricated evidence, or exaggerated transformation claims.
